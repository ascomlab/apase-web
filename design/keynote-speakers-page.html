Keynote Speakers

Join world-renowned experts at APASE 2026 as they explore the future of digital curation, atmospheric science, and computational modeling.

Plenary Session

Artificial Intelligence for Climate Science

Peter Dueben

Head of Earth System Modelling Section, ECMWF

"The integration of machine learning into numerical weather prediction represents the most significant shift in atmospheric science in half a century."

Peter Dueben is the Head of the Earth System Modelling Section at the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF). His research focuses on the development of machine learning applications and high-resolution simulations to improve the accuracy of weather and climate models.

At APASE 2026, Dr. Dueben will discuss the "Digital Twin" approach to Earth observation and how hybrid modeling—combining classical physics with deep learning—is redefining our capacity to predict extreme weather events in a changing climate.

Plenary Session

Next-Gen Wind Farm Dynamics

Jan-Willem van Wingerden

Professor of Wind Energy Control, TU Delft

"Control theory is the invisible hand that will make offshore wind the backbone of the global energy transition."

Jan-Willem van Wingerden leads the Data-Driven Control group at TU Delft. He is a pioneer in the field of wind farm control, specifically focusing on wake steering and the active power control of large-scale wind turbine clusters.

His keynote will highlight recent breakthroughs in closed-loop control for wind farms, demonstrating how real-time data processing and adaptive algorithms can increase energy yield by up to 10% while significantly reducing structural fatigue.
